About

Complexity belongs in the system, not on the person using it.

I started out organizing in Brazil. Through Girl Up Brasil, I led a 25-person team running nationwide campaigns for women and girls, part of a movement whose advocacy has since reached more than 18 million people. The lesson that stuck: the problem is rarely only access. It's making things easier to reach, easier to understand, and less burdensome to navigate.

I bring the same idea to the products I build. An agent that answers from real data instead of guessing, a recommender that matches people to the right nonprofit, a pipeline that turns paper contracts into searchable tables: each one takes on the complexity so the person using it doesn't have to.
